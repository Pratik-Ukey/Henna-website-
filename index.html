Basic Course

Learn the fundamentals of mehndi design from scratch. This course focuses on hand control, cone making, and basic patterns — helping you build a strong foundation in mehndi art.

  • Introduction to mehndi, cone & paste making
  • Line practice (thin, thick, curve, zigzag)
  • Different types of dots, shapes, and checks
  • Shading designs and basic leaf patterns
  • Floral and bent designs
  • Arabic and finger mehndi patterns

Fees₹999

Basic to Advanced

For those who already know the basics, this course helps refine your technique and introduces complex designs and styles. Learn how to create stunning, professional-level mehndi art.

  • Advanced line work and check patterns
